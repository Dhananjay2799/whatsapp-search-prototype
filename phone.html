Sources & connections

WhatsApp Checking

Indexed chats, documents and photos. Experimental linked-device connection; full history is optional and not guaranteed.

Files

Import selected files or folders on your computer. Supports text, PDFs, DOCX, XLSX and photos (JPG, PNG, WebP). Original paths appear in results.

npm run import -- "C:\path\to\folder"

Text in photos and scanned PDFs is read with OCR. File changes require reimporting.

Gmail

Email and supported attachments, including available Spam and Trash. Connect each account separately.

Outlook

Personal, school, and work email. Organization approval may be needed.

iPhone Messages Unavailable

This app cannot automatically read your iPhone Messages history. These messages are not included in results.

Cloud drives Not connected

Direct Google Drive, OneDrive, and iCloud connections are not built yet. You can import files downloaded to your computer.
